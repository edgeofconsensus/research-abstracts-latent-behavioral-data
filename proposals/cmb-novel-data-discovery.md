# CMB: exploratory discovery of unmodeled information
Status: hypothesis / research proposal, not a discovery.

## Aim
Investigate whether public CMB maps contain statistically robust residual structures or useful new representations beyond established analyses. Distinguish novel data encodings from new physical phenomena.

## Data and method
Use public Planck and WMAP temperature/polarization maps and corresponding masks, noise simulations and metadata. Reproduce standard angular power spectra and baseline ΛCDM predictions before exploratory analysis. Compare frequency channels, masks, hemispheres, polarization and independent instruments. Account for foregrounds, systematics, look-elsewhere effects and cosmic variance.

## Tests
1. Record data versions, units, masks, coordinate systems and provenance.
2. Produce reproducible baseline power spectra and simulation controls.
3. Pre-register candidate anomaly statistics; use blinded holdout simulations.
4. Replicate on independent channels or experiments; report null findings.

## Deliverables
Dataset manifest, reproducible notebooks, anomaly registry with effect sizes and uncertainty, and explicit PASS/FAIL criteria. No claims of new physics without independent evidence.
